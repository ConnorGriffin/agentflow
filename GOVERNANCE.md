# Governance

AgentFlow is a maintainer-led project. Connor Griffin is the current maintainer
and final decision maker.

## Decisions

Issues and pull requests are the public record for proposals and implementation.
Durable architecture decisions are recorded under `docs/adr/`. The maintainer
sets scope, release timing, compatibility policy, and merge requirements based
on project direction, safety, evidence, and available maintenance capacity.

Consensus is preferred but not required. Silence, sponsorship, contribution
volume, or prior acceptance does not grant decision authority.

## Contributions

Contributions use the same Apache-2.0 inbound and outbound terms and require DCO
1.1 sign-off. The project does not require copyright assignment or a CLA.
Maintainers may close or decline contributions that are unsafe, out of scope,
insufficiently tested, inconsistent with the product direction, or too costly
to maintain.

## Roles and succession

Additional maintainers may be appointed or removed by the current maintainer.
Repository permissions define operational authority. If stewardship changes,
the transition will be recorded publicly in this file and a repository issue.

## Conduct

Participate in good faith. Harassment, threats, doxxing, spam, and disclosure of
private or security-sensitive information are not accepted. The maintainer may
edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation to protect the
project and its contributors.
